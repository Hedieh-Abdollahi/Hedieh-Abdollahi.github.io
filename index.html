About

My research focuses on stellar evolution through observations of variable stars and evolved stellar populations. I combine multi-wavelength observations, time-domain photometry, stellar population analysis, and spectral energy distribution modelling to investigate the physical properties of stars and nearby galaxies.

Research

Variable Stars

RR Lyrae, Cepheids, long-period variables and stellar variability.

AGB Stars

Dust production, circumstellar envelopes and stellar evolution.

Stellar Populations

Resolved stellar populations of Local Group galaxies.

Galaxy Evolution

Star formation histories of nearby dwarf galaxies.
