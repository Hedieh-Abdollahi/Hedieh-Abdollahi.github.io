About

My research combines observational astronomy and data analysis to investigate stellar evolution and galaxy evolution through variable stars and resolved stellar populations. I work with large photometric surveys and space-based observations to study long-period variables, RR Lyrae stars, dust-producing AGB stars, and the star formation histories of Local Group dwarf galaxies. Current interests also include binary evolution, time-domain astronomy, and stellar variability in the era of Gaia, JWST, Rubin Observatory, and ESO facilities.

Research

Variable Stars

My research focuses on the observational properties and evolution of variable stars, including RR Lyrae stars, Cepheids, and long-period variables, using ground- and space-based photometric surveys.

Asymptotic Giant Branch Stars

I investigate dust production, mass loss, and stellar evolution in AGB stars through spectral energy distribution modelling and infrared observations.

Resolved Stellar Populations

Using nearby galaxies as laboratories, I study stellar populations to reconstruct their star formation histories and chemical evolution.

Galaxy Evolution

My work explores the evolution of Local Group dwarf galaxies by combining resolved stellar photometry, variable-star populations, and multi-wavelength data.
