01 / About

About me

I am an astronomer interested in understanding stellar evolution and the evolution of nearby galaxies through the study of variable stars and stellar populations.

My work combines time-domain observations, multi-wavelength photometry, stellar population analysis, and spectral energy distribution modelling to investigate the physical properties and evolutionary histories of stars and galaxies.

02 / Research

Research interests

01
Variable Stars
Stellar variability and the use of variable stars as probes of stellar evolution and stellar populations.
02
AGB Stars & LPVs
Long-period variables and asymptotic giant branch stars, including their evolution and mass-loss processes.
03
Dust & Mass Loss
Circumstellar dust, stellar winds, and the physical processes governing mass loss from evolved stars.
04
Stellar Populations
The properties and evolution of stellar populations using variable stars and multi-wavelength observations.
05
Dwarf Galaxies
The evolution of Local Group dwarf galaxies and their resolved stellar populations.
06
Star Formation Histories
Reconstructing the star formation histories of nearby galaxies through evolved stellar populations.
