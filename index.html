About

My research focuses on the observational and computational study of stellar variability, from long-period variables to short-period RR Lyrae stars. My research combines time-series data, pipeline development, and light-curve analysis to trace the star-formation histories and mass-loss processes of nearby galaxies, and to uncover hidden structure — such as binary companions or secondary pulsation modes.

Research Background

Local Group • Dwarf Galaxies • AGB Stars • Variable Stars • LPVs • RR Lyrae Stars • Dust & Mass Loss
