About Me

I am an astronomer interested in understanding stellar evolution and the evolution of nearby galaxies through the study of variable stars and stellar populations.


My research combines observations across different wavelengths with photometric analysis, time-series data, stellar population studies, and spectral energy distribution modelling.

Research

Variable Stars

Studying stellar variability and using variable stars as probes of stellar evolution and stellar populations.

AGB Stars & LPVs

Investigating long-period variables and asymptotic giant branch stars, including their evolution and mass loss.

Dust & Mass Loss

Exploring circumstellar dust, mass-loss processes, and the environments around evolved stars.

SED Modelling

Using multi-wavelength photometry and spectral energy distributions to study the physical properties of stars.

Dwarf Galaxies

Studying the evolution of Local Group dwarf galaxies and their stellar populations.

Star Formation Histories

Reconstructing the star formation histories of nearby galaxies using evolved stellar populations and variable stars.
